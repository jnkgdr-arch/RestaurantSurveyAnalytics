use and modification without mandatory attribution. Credits and source links
are retained in the site's expandable Photography credits section. Photo
selection illustrates dining settings; it does not portray survey participants
or imply endorsement. No private credentials or third-party runtime image
requests are needed.

and have maximum widths of 800–1200 pixels. Card layout uses consistent panel
dimensions with CSS object-fit cropping and dark gradients. Original files
remain untouched. All nine served images were decoded in Chromium and checked
under a /RestaurantSurveyAnalytics/ path prefix.
